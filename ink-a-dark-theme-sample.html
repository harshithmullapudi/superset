Theme sample

Ink, a dark theme
built for long reading

Ink is a dark theme for interfaces people sit inside for hours. It sets a near-black ground at #0A0D0F and lifts from it in four small steps, so depth comes from lightness and a hairline rather than a shadow, which only reads as a smudge this far down. Text is a warm off-white, #E8E4DC, which carries 15.4:1 against the ground without the bloom that pure white gets on near-black. Ochre is the one load-bearing accent, reserved for primary actions and never spent on decoration; cyan, green, and rose handle focus, success, and danger, each paired with a dimmed fill so a status can tint a surface without shouting from it. What follows is the whole system in the order you would build it: the four surfaces, the palette with every contrast ratio computed live against the base, the serif and sans pairing and its scale, then the buttons, badges, tables, code, and callouts those tokens produce. Every value below is a live token in this document, so the page is its own proof.

Surfaces

Four steps, each a small lift in lightness rather than a shadow. Shadows read as smudges on a dark base; separation comes from the step and a hairline border.

Surface 0

Page ground. #0A0D0F

Surface 1

Cards, code, table head. #111619

Surface 2

Hover, raised rows. #171D21

Surface 3

Popovers, muted chips. #1F262B

Palette

Contrast ratios are computed in the page against the base, and rounded to one decimal. Anything at or above 4.5 clears AA for body text; 3.0 clears it for large text and non-text marks.

Typography

A serif carries the headings and a sans carries the body, so the two levels differ in shape and not only in size. Mono is reserved for values, paths, and labels.
